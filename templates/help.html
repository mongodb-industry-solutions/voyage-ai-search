Voyage AI Search

← Back to Demo

This demo showcases multimodal semantic search powered by Voyage AI with embeddings stored in MongoDB Atlas. Instead of matching exact keywords, the search understands the meaning of your query and returns the most relevant results from the corpus.

The demo uses content that was provided by Financial Times in the scope of multiple Proof of Concepts, conducted in fall 2025. The corpus is relatively small (a few hundred content items) which means that you need to properly plan for your queries such that you indeed get semantically close results. This is not a limitation of Voyage AI capabilities, but just due to the fact that for a lot of queries there simply does not exist anything close in the data set.

Content Types

Use the toggle buttons at the top to switch between the three available content types:

  • Charts – Financial and data charts with descriptive metadata inside the images. This shows how Voyage AI is capable of indexing text that is embedded in images (typical for financial charts and the like) and makes the text queryable. This eliminates the requirement to use OCR software and simplifies the overall architecture. Financial Times was not able before to have users search for this text, which was limiting the power of their web/app content search.
  • Print – Scanned articles from old Print Media. Similar to Charts, also for scans of old Print Media Voyage AI can do a superb job of indexing the scan of a printed page (provided as one JPEG per page for the PoC we conducted) and makes content within searchable. Be advised that only complete pages can be returned. With Voyage AI alone it is not possible to zoom into the page and mark the actual article that was leading to the good match.
  • Podcast – Transcripts from audio episodes. This shows the power of using the transcript of spoken audio media, breaking it into segments of 20 seconds each, and vectorizing these segments for semantic search. This part of the demo is using one podcast episode of Financial Times, where they speak about Donald Trump, tarrifs, and Elon Musk.

How to search

Type a question or topic into the input field at the bottom and press Send. The system will embed your query using Voyage AI and perform a vector similarity search against the selected content type in MongoDB Atlas.

Results appear in the chat window above, ranked by semantic relevance. Furthermore, there's a media view in the right-hand panel that auto-loads the content from the best match. You can refine your query at any time — each search is independent.

Example queries

Not sure where to start? Click one of the example queries shown below the input field. They are tailored to the currently selected content type and give a good impression of what the model can find.

Tips for better results

  • Use natural language — full sentences often work better than single keywords
  • Be specific: "how inflation erodes cash value" outperforms just "inflation"
